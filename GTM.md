# GTM

The exact target user is a technical founder or engineering manager at a 5–50 person AI-enabled SaaS startup that already pays for several AI tools but does not have formal procurement. They are likely spending on Cursor, ChatGPT Team, Claude, Copilot, and API usage because individual contributors adopted tools organically.

Right before they want this tool, they are probably reading their monthly card statement, seeing a finance Slack message about SaaS spend, or scrolling through founder posts about AI tool overload. They may Google phrases like “Cursor business pricing worth it,” “ChatGPT Team vs Plus,” “reduce OpenAI API bill,” or “AI tools for startup developers.”

They hang out in Indie Hackers, r/SaaS, r/startups, r/LocalLLaMA, X founder circles, build-in-public communities, AI engineering Discords, Vercel/Next.js communities, and college/startup WhatsApp groups.

For the first 100 users with $0 paid budget, I would post 10 teardown threads on X: “I audited this fake 8-person AI stack and found $740/mo waste.” Each thread ends with a free audit link. I would DM 50 founders with a specific observation about their product and offer to audit their AI stack manually if they share anonymized spend. I would ask three startup operators from interviews to share the tool in their communities. I would also publish a short Hacker News Show HN post focused on “no login, instant result” rather than Credex.

The unfair distribution channel is Credex’s own knowledge of discounted AI credits. Most spend calculators can say “downgrade.” Credex can say “you may be paying retail for credits that already exist in the market.”

Week-1 traction if this works: 100 completed audits, 35 email captures, 8 consultation bookings, and 2 leads with more than $1,000/month potential savings.
