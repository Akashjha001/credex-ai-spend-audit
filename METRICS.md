# Metrics

The North Star metric is qualified audits completed per week. This matches the product because the tool is not a daily app; it creates value when a startup completes an audit and sees a savings estimate. A completed audit also creates the opportunity for lead capture and consultation booking.

Three input metrics drive the North Star: landing-to-audit-start rate, audit-start-to-completion rate, and completed-audit-to-email-capture rate. If users do not start, the promise is unclear. If they start but do not finish, the form is too long or confusing. If they complete but do not leave email, the result is not valuable enough.

I would instrument page view, audit start, tool added, audit completed, result shared, email captured, and consultation clicked. I would also segment by total savings bucket: under $100, $100–$500, and over $500.

A pivot decision triggers if 200 targeted visitors produce fewer than 20 completed audits or if high-savings audits produce under 5% consultation clicks. That would mean either the audience is wrong, the calculator lacks trust, or the savings are not painful enough.
