# Economics

Assumption: a converted Credex customer buys discounted AI infrastructure credits and Credex earns a gross margin or broker fee. If an average qualified startup spends $3,000/month on AI tools and Credex can move $1,500/month into discounted credits with a 15% effective margin, the monthly gross profit is $225. Annual gross profit is $2,700. I would value a converted lead at roughly $1,000–$1,500 initially after applying risk and churn discounts.

Channel CAC estimates from the GTM plan: founder DMs cost mostly time, so cash CAC is $0 and time CAC may be 20 minutes per qualified audit. Community posts and Show HN have $0 cash CAC. If 100 users complete audits, 35 leave email, 8 book calls, and 2 buy credits, the audit-to-purchase conversion rate is 2%. With a $1,200 conservative lead value, that creates $2,400 value from 100 audits.

Profitability threshold: if consultation close rate is 25% and booked-call rate from completed audit is 8%, then purchase rate is 2%. At $1,200 value per customer, each completed audit is worth $24 on average. That leaves room for future paid acquisition, but the first version should stay organic until conversion data is proven.

For this tool to drive $1M ARR in 18 months, Credex would need about $83k/month recurring gross revenue contribution. At $225/month gross profit per converted customer, that means roughly 370 active converted customers. If 2% of completed audits convert, Credex needs about 18,500 completed audits over 18 months, or about 1,030 completed audits per month. That is possible only if the product has strong organic sharing, SEO from pricing pages, and a sales motion for high-savings users.
