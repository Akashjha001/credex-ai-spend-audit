## FAQ

**Is this free?** Yes, the audit is free.

**Do I need to create an account?** No. Email is requested only after the audit result is shown.

**How accurate are the savings?** Savings are based on public pricing, selected plans, team size, and rule-based recommendations.

**Will my company details be public?** No. Public audit URLs strip email and company-identifying fields.

**What happens if my savings are high?** Credex may be able to help you capture more savings through discounted AI credits.
